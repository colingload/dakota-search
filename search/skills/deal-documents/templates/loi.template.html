4. Working Capital

The Company will be delivered at closing with a normalized level of net working capital sufficient to operate in the ordinary course (a “peg” to be agreed based on trailing twelve-month averages once the balance sheet and A/R and A/P aging are provided). The Purchase Price adjusts dollar-for-dollar for any shortfall or excess against the agreed peg.

5. Financing Contingency

This transaction is contingent on Buyer obtaining SBA 7(a) acquisition financing on commercially reasonable terms. Buyer is pre-qualified and will pursue financing diligently in parallel with confirmatory diligence. If Buyer is unable to obtain SBA financing through no fault of Buyer, this LOI shall terminate and neither party shall have further obligation to the other.

6. Confirmatory Due Diligence

Closing is conditioned on Buyer’s satisfactory completion of confirmatory due diligence, including (without limitation):

  • Quality-of-earnings review confirming SDE off three years of business tax returns and an accrual restatement of the cash-basis P&L.
  • Itemized add-back schedule with supporting documentation.
  • Year-end balance sheets, A/R and A/P aging, and debt schedule.
  • Customer concentration analysis — revenue by customer (top 10), recurring-contract vs. project split, and contract renewal terms.
  • Assignability of material customer contracts, subcontractor agreements, and the facility lease (SBA requires 10+ years of lease term including renewals).
  • Owner compensation and the function and replacement cost of all non-owner labor.
  • Customary legal, insurance, licensing, environmental, and litigation review.

Seller agrees to provide the foregoing promptly upon execution of this LOI to enable an efficient process.
