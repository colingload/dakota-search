## Standard Diligence Questions (reference list)

**Financial:**
- 3 years + TTM P&Ls (monthly if available)
- 3 years of tax returns (business)
- Current balance sheet
- A/R aging, A/P aging (current)
- Bank statements (last 12 months)
- Customer list with revenue by customer (top 20)
- Revenue by product/service line
- Owner compensation + all add-backs with documentation

**Operational:**
- Org chart + employee roster (tenure, comp, role)
- Key vendor list + terms
- Customer contracts (sample + top 10)
- Lease agreements
- Equipment list + age
- Software/systems stack

**Legal / Risk:**
- Pending litigation
- Insurance policies
- Regulatory licenses
- IP / trademarks
- Environmental (if applicable)

**Seller:**
- Detailed reason for sale
- Post-close involvement preference + duration
- Training period
- Non-compete willingness + geography
- Willingness to carry seller note + terms
